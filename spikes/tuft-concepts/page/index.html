Branch design-updates

Tuft and the Keep Reading row

Two design passes. Tuft gets concept art from the new picture models plus a redrawn, still-animated SVG. Keep Reading gets three new layouts to choose from.

Part 1

A higher-fidelity Tuft

Can the picture models draw an animatable Tuft?

Not directly. GPT Image and Nano Banana make flat pictures (pixels). They can't make the separate layers we animate: hat, wings, eyes, tail.

So what are they good for?

Concept art. They're great for exploring the look quickly. Once we pick a direction, I redraw it as layered SVG so the bobbing, blinking and wiggling keep working.

Another option

A hybrid. Use a painted Tuft for big moments (Welcome, Meet Tuft, empty states) with a gentle float and sparkle. Keep the SVG for small sizes and for the poses that need moving parts.

What was going wrong

The two “ears” were his wings: tall ovals set high on his sides, right under the hat brim. The “guitar neck” was meant to be his long tail; Tuft is a long-tailed tit (shima enaga), the little Japanese “snow fairy” bird. The extra piece on the hat was a curled tip with a gold pom-pom. Each one came out ambiguous, so I told the models exactly what to avoid.

Concept art: three styles, two models each

Expression sheets

The same poses the app uses today: wave, read, write, sleep, cheer, think. Both models kept him consistent across poses, so a painted set of hero images is realistic.

SVG rebuild, still animated

A redraw of the Flat vector concept in code. It's already on the branch, so every screen that shows Tuft would pick it up. Watch him bob and blink; the tail now gives a little flick.

Before
After
  • Wings, not ears. Smaller wings sit lower on his sides, with scalloped feather tips and a dark edge. The far wing is smaller, so he looks turned slightly to one side.
  • A readable tail. Alternating dark and pale feathers fan up from behind him, like the concepts.
  • One clean hat. A single floppy tip with no pom-pom, a wider brim with an underside, a gold band and a soft highlight. It also fixes a bug where the hat's wiggle animation wiped out its tilt.
  • A real beak and softer blush. A small wedge beak with a highlight, gradient cheeks and a second catch-light in each eye.
